Methodology and limitations

Entry and exit use executable quote sides: BUY enters at Ask and exits at Bid; SELL enters at Bid and exits at Ask. MFE/MAE use executable-side movement. Spread is therefore already embedded and is not subtracted twice. Horizons are 5, 15, 30 and 60 seconds. The console repeats every replay at assumed signal-to-entry delays of 0, 250, 500, 1,000, 2,000 and 3,000 ms. The 0 ms case is an idealised baseline, not a realistic manual fill. Entry uses the first quote at or after the configured delay; it must arrive within a further 1.5 seconds or the signal is skipped. An exit quote must arrive within 1.5 seconds after its target time or that horizon is skipped.

The archive is split chronologically 60/20/20. Signals are assigned by their timestamp to one partition only. No signal or exit is allowed to borrow ticks from another partition. The report records all evaluated outcomes and skipped horizons. Hit rate is not profitability. Commission, slippage beyond recorded Bid/Ask, network latency, partial fills and broker-specific execution are not modelled. Biquote/reference-provider results cannot establish Exness fill parity.

Held-out test results must not be used to tune parameters. Run the strategy on training data, select parameters on validation data, then evaluate the locked strategy once on held-out test data. This console replays supplied signal logs; it does not itself train or optimise a strategy.
