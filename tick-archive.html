Archives and raw ticks stay in this browser's IndexedDB; they are not uploaded to Vercel or Neon. Export a copy for backup. Each import receives a content-derived SHA-256 identifier. Chronological partitions are fixed at 60% train, 20% validation and 20% held-out test. Do not tune against the held-out test partition.

Validation boundary

This module validates and versions raw archives only. It does not claim a strategy backtest has run. Replay execution, transaction-cost modelling and broker-parity calibration remain separate stages. A reference-provider archive cannot prove Exness executable prices or fills.
